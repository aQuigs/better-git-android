# Privacy policy

SafeGit collects no personal data.

- The app talks only to the git hosts you add (for example GitHub), to fetch and push your repositories.
- It reads and writes only the folders you choose to sync.
- Your sign-in token stays on your phone, encrypted with the Android Keystore, and is sent only to the host it belongs to.
- There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts of our own and no third-party services.
- Uninstalling the app deletes its settings and token; your synced folders stay where they are.
